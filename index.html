The problem

The meds take weight off.
Not all of it is fat.

When appetite drops, protein drops. When protein and training drop, muscle goes with the fat — and muscle is what keeps you strong, lean and keeps the weight from coming back.

Your prescriber handles the medication. Nobody's handling the training, the food, or the follow-through. That's the job here.
